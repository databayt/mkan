# Port Sudan listings — unit level

**One record here is one property.** A studio in حي المطار مربع ٨ at 1,200/month is a row.

This is the companion to [`../../market-research/port-sudan/rental-leads.json`](../../market-research/port-sudan/rental-leads.json), which is one record per *business* — 43 hotels, furnished-apartment operators and brokers. That dataset answered *who holds inventory in this city*. This one answers *what is actually available*. The two join on phone number, which is the only identifier this market shares across platforms.

Its existence was called for by the business-level discovery log, which named the gap as its own #1:

> Individual landlord ads are catalogued, not collected. Each such ad is one landlord with one flat — genuine Mkan supply, but a different unit of work from the business-level dataset this brief asked for. Collecting them is the obvious next project.

## Regenerating

```bash
pnpm crm:ps-listings
```

Deterministic: same sources in, byte-identical files out. **Do not hand-edit `listings.json` or `listings.md`** — edit `../../market-research/port-sudan/sources/` and regenerate, the same rule the business dataset follows.

## Sources

| Layer | Where | Gives | Cannot give |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Classifieds | `sources/alsoug-adverts.json` | The richest structure available anywhere — rooms, baths, floor, area, furnished, generator, price, currency | **Never a phone.** alsoug hides the advertiser's number behind a login gate |
| Operator marketing | `sources/channels-expansion.json` | Units a business advertises itself, with the phones it publishes | Rarely a price |
| Facebook groups | `sources/facebook-groups.json` | Volume. One group alone has 126.6K members | Needs a logged-in browser session |
| WhatsApp groups | `whatsapp-exports/*.txt` | Free text with inline phones and a real timestamp per message | Needs the operator to export each chat |

A missing source does not fail the build — it is reported in `_sources_absent_this_run` and printed to the console, because a silent skip reads as completeness.

## The rule this inherits

Nothing enters from recall. Every record carries either a URL that was fetched or a `file:line` that can be opened, and the generator **throws** rather than emit a row without one. A plausible-sounding flat nobody can trace back to a post is worse than an empty dataset, because someone will pick up the phone and call it.

## Zones

District assignment resolves against `zones.json` (45 zones, generated by `pnpm crm:ps-zones`) plus `sources/zone-alias-extensions.json` for spellings the canon lacks but adverts actually use — the bare `الثورة`, the single-alef `سللاب`. Every extension carries the advert it was observed in.

**A listing that cannot be placed stays unplaced** and is listed in `_unplaced`. That is deliberate: the zone density map is what the acquisition team plans routes around, so a confidently wrong row there does more damage than a blank one. `المدينة الصناعية` and "next to the Air Force College" are both currently unplaced for exactly this reason.

## One classification trap worth knowing

`ارض` ("land") is a prefix of `ارضي` / `ارضية` ("ground floor"). A land-first classifier reads **شقه ارضية للايجار** — a ground-floor *flat* — as a plot of land, and **عمارة … ارضي + اول** — a *building* — as land too. Both were misclassified on this generator's first run. The land pattern is now anchored to reject the suffix, and structure words are tested first.

## Privacy posture — read before committing this

The business dataset is committed to the repo, and its discovery log justifies that: it holds **business contact information published by businesses that are soliciting contact** — a hotel's phone number on its own Google listing.

**This dataset is not that.** Rows flagged `individual_poster` are private individuals who posted a flat to a group, not businesses advertising to the public. They sit closer to `scripts/crm/.data/` — which is gitignored precisely because it holds scraped personal host data — than to `rental-leads.json`.

A `.gitignore` is therefore shipped alongside this README covering `listings.json` and `listings.md`. If you decide to commit them anyway, that is a deliberate call to make explicitly, not one to arrive at by not noticing.

Also: collecting Facebook group content at volume is against Facebook's terms of service, regardless of the group being public. That is a business risk to weigh, not a technical obstacle.
