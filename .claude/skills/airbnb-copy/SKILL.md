# Airbnb Copy — The Master Rental Copywriting Voice

Turn raw home specifications into compelling, warm, and clear listing copy that sounds **authentically like Airbnb**. Grounded in Mkan's scraped dataset of 121+ Sudan Airbnb listings (`data/airbnb-sudan-listings.md`).

---

## 1. The Core Airbnb Voice Doctrine

Airbnb copy is **not a real estate listing**, **not a hotel brochure**, and **not a sales pitch**. It is a **thoughtful, proud host welcoming a guest into their space**.

| Dimension | Real Estate Broker (❌ Never) | Airbnb Voice (✅ Always) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tone** | Transactional, formal, hype-heavy ("Spectacular investment opportunity!!") | Warm, conversational, hospitable, grounded ("Step out onto the balcony with your morning tea.") |
| **Pacing** | Bulleted spec dump without context | Sensory walkthrough following the guest's physical journey through the door |
| **Honesty** | Conceals quirks, inflates sizes | Radically transparent ("Quiet 3rd floor walkup with standby generator for guaranteed peace of mind") |
| **Language** | Clunky machine translation | Arabic-first (warm, natural Fusha with authentic Sudanese warmth) + mirrored concise English |

---

## 2. Listing Structure: The 4-Beat Airbnb Layout

Every listing description must follow this standard Airbnb markdown structure:

```markdown
[Opening Hook — 2 to 3 sentences capturing the experience, natural light, and immediate peace of mind (power/water/wifi)]

**The space** (المساحة)
[Room-by-room walkthrough: living area, bedrooms, bed configurations, kitchen setup, balcony/veranda view]

**Guest access** (وصول الضيوف)
[Explicit boundaries: entire apartment, private entrance, dedicated parking, elevator/stairs]

**Other things to note** (ملاحظات أخرى)
[Essential practical details: backup generator schedule/automatic switch, standby water tanks, AC type, direct host contact]
```

## 3. Title Formulas (Bilingual Parity)

### English Formula:
`[Sensory Adjective / Hook] + [Bedrooms / Unit Type] + [Neighborhood / Landmark / City]`
- *Example*: `"Sunlit Coastal 2-Bedroom Apartment in Hayy Al-Shati"`
- *Example*: `"Modern Studio with 24/7 Generator & WiFi · Salbona"`
- *Example*: `"Spacious Family Flat near Airport District · Port Sudan"`

### Arabic Formula:
`[نوع الوحدة / الشقة] + [الميزة البارزة أو عدد الغرف] + [الحي / المدينة]`
- *Example*: `"شقة ساحلية مشرقة بغرفتين وصالة — حي الشاطئ، بورتسودان"`
- *Example*: `"استوديو عصري بمولد كهربائي وإنترنت سريع — سلبونا"`
- *Example*: `"شقة عائلية واسعة بالقرب من حي المطار — بورتسودان"`

## 6. Strict Anti-Patterns (Checklist before Shipping Copy)

1. ❌ **No robotic / translation-ese**: Avoid literal translations like "وحدة الإيجار" or "الممتلكات تحتوي". Use natural Arabic: "شقة مفروشة", "البيت يحتوي على".
2. ❌ **No fake hype / fabricated claims**: Never claim "Ultra 7-star luxury" or "Cheapest in Sudan". State the comforts truthfully.
3. ❌ **No missing infrastructure assurances**: In Sudan rentals, guests immediately look for power (generator) and water. Always address these in the first paragraph and notes.
4. ❌ **No passive voice**: Write active, welcoming copy ("Enjoy morning tea on the terrace", not "Tea can be consumed on the balcony").
5. ❌ **Keep mkan.sd contact-first**: In Phase 1, remind guests to connect directly with the host via phone.
