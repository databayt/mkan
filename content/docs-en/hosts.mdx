## How your home reaches mkan

Some homes are added by the mkan team after finding them advertised online (on Airbnb or TikTok, for example); others are added by their owners directly.

## Claiming your account

If the team added your home, you receive a claim link. The link is the key to your account: open it, confirm your details, and the listing is yours.

<Callout title="Your account number">
  Your account number is the first four digits of your listing code — the same digits as your `NNNN@mkan.org` login.
</Callout>

## Running your listing

From the hosting dashboard (`/hosting`) you edit photos, description, price and availability. When the listing is ready, the team publishes it on the site.
