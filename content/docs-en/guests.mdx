## Search

<Steps>

### Open the listings page

Go to `/listings` to see available homes, and use the filters to set the place, dates, guests and price.

### Open a listing

A listing page carries the photos, description, amenities and an availability calendar. Picking dates on the calendar fills the reserve box.

### Reach out or book

Send your request from the listing page. If you have a listing code (such as `0002-05`), type it into the site to land on that listing directly.

</Steps>

## Search by describing it

The `/find` page lets you describe what you want in your own words, and mkan suggests matching homes.
