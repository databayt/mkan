## 1. Scrape

mkan reads the rental ads brokers post and pulls out the home and the person behind it.

<Mermaid chart={`flowchart TD
  A(["Known broker accounts"]) --> B["Discover new videos"]
  B --> C["Fetch: 8 frames, on-screen text, audio transcript"]
  C --> D["Read: Claude Haiku or Groq"]
  D --> E{"A rental? In Kigali? Price or rooms? Confident?"}
  E -->|No| X(["Skipped, with the reason"])
  E -->|Yes| F{"Same rooms, price and place?"}
  F -->|Yes| G(["Note on the existing home"])
  F -->|No| H(["Home and host in the CRM"])`} />

- **Sources:** TikTok (the main one), property sites (houseinrwanda, imali, rwandahouseland), and Facebook by hand. Agencies are found on Google Maps.
- **Schedule (Kigali time):** TikTok 06:00 and 15:00, property sites 06:45 and 18:45, agencies 11:00.
- **New accounts** are proposed by search and added only when the team says so.

## 2. Enhance

Makes the ad a home that can be shown and searched.

<Mermaid chart={`flowchart TD
  A(["Home in vetting"]) --> B{"Monthly? Sensible price? Broker's number known?"}
  B -->|No| X(["Stays on the board only"])
  B -->|Yes| C["Draft on the site, linked to the broker"]
  C --> D["Zone and terms: deposit, commission, water"]
  D --> E{"Price missing?"}
  E -->|Yes| F["Ask the broker on WhatsApp"]
  F --> D
  E -->|No| G["Photos: AVIF and WebP sizes, blur placeholder"]
  G --> H["Daily clean-up: duplicates, phone numbers, deleted videos"]
  H --> I(["Draft ready"])`} />

- **Schedule:** drafts move with every scrape and at 09:00, price asks 09:30, clean-up 07:00, photo sizes hourly.
- **A draft** is not shown to visitors in the listings, but the Mkan agent finds it and shows it as "availability not confirmed yet".

## 3. Post

Two ways out, each with its own decision.

<Mermaid chart={`flowchart TD
  A(["Draft ready"]) --> B{"Team set it LIVE in the CRM?"}
  B -->|Yes| C["The site publishes it and mints a listing code"]
  C --> D(["Live on mkan.sd"])
  A --> E{"Broker agreed? 4+ photos? Recent ad?"}
  E -->|Yes| F["Video prepared every morning at 07:00"]
  F --> G["Uploaded to @mkan.rw, 17:00–21:30"]
  G --> H(["On TikTok, then rests 30 days"])
  D --> I["Hourly sync back to the CRM board"]`} />

## 4. Chat

The Mkan agent reads published homes and recent drafts together, finds the tenant a match on the site or on WhatsApp, then asks the broker and arranges the viewing. Details and the video in [Mkan agent](/en/docs/chatbot).

## For the team

- Scrape: `pnpm crm:tiktok`, `pnpm crm:web-ads run`, `pnpm crm:fb-rentals`, `pnpm crm:agencies`.
- Enhance: `pnpm crm:tiktok-mirror`, `pnpm crm:zones`, `pnpm crm:price-ask`, `pnpm crm:hygiene`, `pnpm images:variants`.
- Post: `publishState` in the CRM, `pnpm tt:run` and `pnpm tt:upload` for the TikTok page, `pnpm crm:sync-up`.
- Schedules run on Hermes and launchd on the Mac, and GitHub Actions for photos.
- The old Port Sudan and Airbnb commands (`crm:scrape`, `crm:import`, `crm:publish`, `crm:gate`) are not part of the Kigali flow.
